The Infinite Gallery

∞

Tilt your device to roll the marker through the room and view the pieces on display. Forward tilt rolls you forward, back tilts you back, and side to side turns the room around you.

↑ Tilt forward — roll ahead
↓ Tilt back — roll behind
← Tilt left — roll left
→ Tilt right — roll right
On desktop, use arrow keys or WASD instead of tilt.
